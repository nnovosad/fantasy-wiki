# Sports.ru Fantasy Rules


**Summary**: Standardized rules for creating and managing a team in fantasy football tournaments on the Sports.ru platform.


**Sources**: [[raw/leagues/england/rules.md]], [[raw/leagues/spain/rules.md]], [[raw/leagues/italy/rules.md]], [[raw/leagues/france/rules.md]], [[raw/leagues/turkey/rules.md]], [[raw/leagues/championship/rules.md]]


**Last updated**: 2026-08-18


---


The rules for fantasy football on Sports.ru are standardized across major European leagues and the English Championship. A manager's goal is to assemble a squad of 15 players within a budget to earn points based on their real-world performances.


## Team Creation


- **Roster Size**: Exactly 15 players (2 Goalkeepers, 5 Defenders, 5 Midfielders, 3 Forwards).
- **Budget**: 100 million credits.
- **Club Limit**: No more than 3 players from a single club (source: rules.md).


## Basic Mechanics


- **[[scoring-and-points]]**: Players earn points for goals, assists, clean sheets, and ball recoveries.
- **[[transfers-and-deadlines]]**: Managers can make a limited number of changes to their squad before each round.
- **[[auto-substitutions-concept]]**: If a starter does not play, a substitute automatically takes their place if legal formation requirements are met.
- **Captaincy**: Each team must have a captain (whose points are doubled) and a vice-captain (source: rules.md).


## League Participation


Managers automatically join a "Fan League" for their favorite team and can create or join private "User Leagues". "Rookie Leagues" are also created starting from Round 2 for those who join late (source: rules.md).
