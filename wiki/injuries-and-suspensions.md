# Injuries and Suspensions


**Summary**: Rules and resources for managing player absences due to physical injury or disciplinary sanctions.


**Sources**: [[fantasy-optimial-strategy.md]]


**Last updated**: 2026-08-18


---


Tracking injuries and suspensions is critical because they are objective facts that directly impact team availability (source: fantasy-optimial-strategy.md).


## Decision Rules


- **One-match absence**: If a player is suspended or injured for only one game, they can be kept in the squad and placed on the bench, especially if they are a "must-have" player. Selling and then re-buying them wastes valuable transfers (source: fantasy-optimial-strategy.md).
- **Multi-match absence**: If a player is expected to miss more than one match, they should always be sold, even if they are a key player.


## Must-Have Players


A **must-have** is a player who is essential for any fantasy team in a particular championship. Managing these players carefully during brief absences is vital to maintaining overall performance (source: fantasy-optimial-strategy.md).


## Resources


- **Second Screens**: Apps like MyScore and Forza Football provide pre-match injury and suspension data.
- **Specialized Sites**: Betinf.com and similar "injuries and suspensions" search results.
- **News Searches**: Searching for "[player name] injuries" to determine the severity and expected recovery time (source: fantasy-optimial-strategy.md).
