# Lineups and Preparation


**Summary**: Methods for analyzing team forms and finding official or probable lineups before the transfer deadline.


**Sources**: [[fantasy-optimial-strategy.md]]


**Last updated**: 2026-08-18


---


Preparation is a key component of OFS, involving both long-term pre-season analysis and short-term pre-round checks.


## Pre-season Analysis


To prepare for the start of a season, managers should:
1.  **Analyze 5-6 pre-season matches** for each team, focusing on the quality of the opposition.
2.  **Identify "Iron" Starters**: Pay special attention to the *last two matches* before the season starts. Players who played in both are most likely to be in the starting XI for Round 1 (source: fantasy-optimial-strategy.md).
3.  **Find "Jokers"**: Look for high-potential players from smaller teams with favorable early schedules.


## Finding Official Lineups


The most reliable way to find official lineups just before the deadline is via **Twitter**. Managers should search for specific "fixed" players from a team's starting XI (e.g., search for "trapp silva" for PSG) and refresh frequently to see the first reports from official accounts or major journalists (source: fantasy-optimial-strategy.md).


## Resources


Recommended resources for probable lineups include:
- **Spain**: AS (resultados.as.com)
- **France**: L'Equipe, Ligue1.com
- **England (Championship)**: BBC, SkySports
- **Italy**: Gazzetta dello Sport (source: fantasy-optimial-strategy.md)
