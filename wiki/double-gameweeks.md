# Double Gameweeks


**Summary**: Strategies for handling rescheduled matches and rounds where teams play twice ("sparkas").


**Sources**: [[fantasy-optimial-strategy.md]]


**Last updated**: 2026-08-18


---


Rescheduled matches create significant opportunities and risks in fantasy football. Identifying these events early is crucial for long-term planning (source: fantasy-optimial-strategy.md).


## Rescheduled Matches


If a team skips a round, their players should generally be sold, with two exceptions:
1.  **Benchable Defenders**: If a cheap defender is missing a round but has a favorable match in the next one, they can be kept on the bench.
2.  **Low-cost Midfielders**: Similar to defenders, if keeping them doesn't block necessary transfers (source: fantasy-optimial-strategy.md).


## Double Gameweeks ("Sparkas")


A **"sparka"** occurs when a team plays two matches within a single fantasy round.


- **Top Teams**: Managers should maximize the number of players from a top team playing twice and almost always captain one of them, even if one of their matches is difficult. The mathematical expectation (EV) is generally higher for two games (source: fantasy-optimial-strategy.md).
- **Non-top Teams**: Caution is advised. While having 1-2 players as jokers is good, over-investing in weak teams just because they play twice can leave the squad with "toxic" assets that are hard to sell later. Defensive players are safer in these cases as they are less likely to be rotated (source: fantasy-optimial-strategy.md).


## Monitoring Tools


Managers should regularly check the calendar displayed under their team and use Telegram bots or "second screen" apps to stay informed about deadline shifts and rescheduled games (source: fantasy-optimial-strategy.md).
