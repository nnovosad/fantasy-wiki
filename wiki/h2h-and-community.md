**Summary**: The role of Head-to-Head leagues, franchises, and the social aspects of the fantasy community.


**Sources**: [[fantasy-optimial-strategy.md]]


**Last updated**: 2026-08-18


---


Fantasy football on Sports.ru is not just about the overall standings; it also involves a vibrant community and competitive sub-games like H2H (source: fantasy-optimial-strategy.md).


## H2H (Head-to-Head)


**H2H** is a popular application for fantasy where managers compete directly against each other in a league format. While highly engaging, OFS recommends treating H2H as a "fun addition" rather than letting it dictate transfer strategy, which should always prioritize the overall standings (source: fantasy-optimial-strategy.md).


## Franchises


A **franchise** is a group of fantasy managers who collaborate, share information, and compete together. Joining or founding a franchise is strongly recommended as it:
- **Accelerates Analysis**: Participants can divide the work of analyzing different leagues or teams.
- **Improves Skills**: Collaboration with other dedicated managers helps refine strategies and identify technical errors (source: fantasy-optimial-strategy.md).


## Etiquette and "Fantasy Tone"


The community thrives on respectful interaction. Recommended practices include:
- **Respectful Critique**: Provide arguments rather than vague criticism.
- **Avoiding False Information**: Do not spread "fake news" about injuries or lineups to trick opponents.
- **Support**: Offer advice to franchise teammates *before* a round rather than criticizing them after it (source: fantasy-optimial-strategy.md).
