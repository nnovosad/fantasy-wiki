# Squad Management


**Summary**: Strategies for optimizing the 15-player roster, planning transfers, and managing the bench.


**Sources**: [[fantasy-optimial-strategy.md]]


**Last updated**: 2026-08-18


---


Effective squad management in OFS involves maximizing the utility of every slot in the team, including the substitutes.


## The 15-Active-Player Strategy


While some managers prefer "dead" bench players to save budget, OFS recommends having **15 playing footballers**, including a backup goalkeeper (source: fantasy-optimial-strategy.md). This provides:
- **Stability**: Better coverage if a starter is unexpectedly benched.
- **Flexibility**: The ability to rotate players based on the difficulty of their upcoming matches.


## Planning Horizon


Managers should prepare their squad for the **next 2 gameweeks**, rather than just the immediate one or looking too far ahead (5+ weeks). Planning beyond two rounds is considered potentially counterproductive and confusing (source: fantasy-optimial-strategy.md).


## Bench Usage


- **First Substitute**: Should be a player who is a strong candidate for the current round.
- **Second and Third Substitutes**: Optimally held for the *following* round. This effectively increases the number of "available" transfers for the next week (source: fantasy-optimial-strategy.md).


## Goalkeeper Strategy


Instead of one "premium" keeper and a non-playing backup, it is recommended to have **two mid-priced goalkeepers** (e.g., ~5.5 credits each). This ensures that in almost every round, at least one has a favorable match, and they provide insurance for each other (source: fantasy-optimial-strategy.md).
