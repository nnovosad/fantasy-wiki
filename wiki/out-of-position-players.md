# Out of Position (OOP) Players


**Summary**: Players who are listed at one position in the fantasy game but play a more offensive role on the pitch.


**Sources**: [[fantasy-optimial-strategy.md]]


**Last updated**: 2026-08-18


---


**Out of Position (OOP)** players are highly valuable because they can earn points based on their fantasy classification while performing actions (like scoring goals) typically associated with more offensive roles (source: fantasy-optimial-strategy.md).


## Examples of OOP


- **Defenders as Midfielders**: A player listed as a defender who plays as an attacking midfielder or winger (e.g., Raphaël Guerreiro). They can earn points for a "clean sheet" while having high goal/assist potential.
- **Midfielders as Forwards**: A player listed as a midfielder who plays as a central striker (e.g., Dries Mertens during certain periods).


## Identifying OOP Players


- **Lineup Analysis**: Carefully checking team formations and actual player positioning during matches.
- **Heat Maps**: Using resources like WhoScored to see where a player actually spends time with the ball.
- **Pre-season Observation**: Starters in new roles are often identified during pre-season friendlies (source: fantasy-optimial-strategy.md).


## Strategic Value


In an ideal squad, a manager should aim to have multiple attacking defenders who can provide both clean sheet points and offensive returns. Identifying these players earlier than the general fantasy community is a significant competitive advantage (source: fantasy-optimial-strategy.md).
