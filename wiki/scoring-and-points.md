# Scoring and Points


**Summary**: The detailed point system used by Sports.ru to evaluate player performance in fantasy football.


**Sources**: [[raw/leagues/england/rules.md]]


**Last updated**: 2026-08-18


---


Fantasy points are awarded based on real actions on the pitch. The value of these actions varies depending on a player's listed position (source: rules.md).


## Positive Points


- **Playing Time**: Points for appearing in a match (typically 1 point for starting or playing).
- **Goals**: 
    - Forward: +4 points
    - Midfielder: +5 points
    - Defender/Goalkeeper: +6 points
- **Assists**: +3 points for all positions.
- **Fantasy-Assist**: +3 points for indirect assists (e.g., earning a penalty, shots leading to own goals, shots saved then rebounded). A player cannot assist themselves (source: rules.md).
- **Clean Sheet**: +4 points for defenders and goalkeepers if they play 60+ minutes and their team concedes no goals while they are on the pitch.
- **Ball Recoveries**: +1 point for every 3 successful ball recoveries (interceptions, tackles where possession is retained, or pick-ups).


## Negative Points


- **Goals Conceded**: -1 point for every 2 goals conceded (defenders and goalkeepers only).
- **Yellow Card**: -1 point.
- **Red Card**: -3 points.
- **Missed Penalty**: -2 points.
- **Own Goal**: -2 points.
- **Causing a Penalty**: -2 points for the player who committed the foul (source: rules.md).


## Data Verification


Statistics are provided by an external provider. There is a **72-hour freeze period** after the last match of a round, during which stats may be updated or corrected (source: rules.md).
