# Fantasy Optimal Strategy (OFS)


**Summary**: A comprehensive guide to a systematic, mathematical approach to seasonal fantasy football on Sports.ru, authored by sazam.


**Sources**: [[fantasy-optimial-strategy.md]]


**Last updated**: 2026-08-18


---


The **Optimal Fantasy Strategy (OFS)** is a framework designed to maximize success in seasonal fantasy football by treating the game as a mathematical exercise similar to poker (GTO). It emphasizes data-driven decisions over intuition and focuses on long-term consistency across multiple tournaments.


## Key Components


- **[[optimal-fantasy-strategy-concept]]**: The core philosophy of treating fantasy as a mathematical game.
- **[[lineups-and-preparation]]**: How to analyze pre-season and prepare for upcoming rounds.
- **[[squad-management]]**: Efficiently using all 15 slots and planning transfers for a 2-week horizon.
- **[[statistics-and-metrics]]**: Utilizing metrics like Shots per Game (SpG) and Key Passes (KeyP).
- **[[injuries-and-suspensions]]**: Objective rules for handling player absences.
- **[[captaincy-strategy]]**: Selecting high-potential captains based on odds and form.
- **[[out-of-position-players]]**: Identifying players playing more offensively than their listed role.
- **[[set-piece-takers]]**: The value of penalty and free-kick takers.
- **[[jokers-and-risk-management]]**: Balancing risk with differential picks and avoiding technical errors like "styks".
- **[[double-gameweeks]]**: Strategic handling of rescheduled matches.
- **[[h2h-and-community]]**: Integrating with the fantasy community and H2H leagues.


## Success Metrics


A consistent "championship pace" is identified as averaging approximately 55 points per gameweek across most major European leagues (source: fantasy-optimial-strategy.md).
