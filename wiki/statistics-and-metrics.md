# Statistics and Metrics


**Summary**: The use of objective performance data to identify high-potential players and avoid statistical traps.


**Sources**: [[fantasy-optimial-strategy.md]]


**Last updated**: 2026-08-18


---


Statistics are the "alphabet" of preparation for a fantasy round. They allow managers to identify "jokers" that others might miss (source: fantasy-optimial-strategy.md).


## Key Metrics


- **SpG (Shots per Game)**: A primary indicator of goal-scoring potential. Shots from inside the penalty area or on target are most valuable.
- **KeyP (Key Passes)**: An indicator of assist potential.
- **Goals and Assists**: The ultimate output, but often lagging indicators compared to shots and key passes.


## The "Ziyech Effect"


Managers must be wary of the **"Ziyech Effect"**: some players may have very high shot counts but low conversion rates because they shoot primarily from a distance. While frequent, these shots are unlikely to result in goals (source: fantasy-optimial-strategy.md).


## Bookmaker Odds


Betting odds are considered a highly competent and time-saving resource for evaluating team and player potential. They should be used to quickly narrow down options for each round (source: fantasy-optimial-strategy.md).


## Meaningless Statistics


OFS advises ignoring certain types of data that do not correlate with fantasy success:
- **Head-to-head history**: Historical results between teams are often irrelevant due to changes in players, managers, and motivation.
- **Superstitions**: Playing on certain days of the week or in specific cities.
- **Deep stats**: Tackles, dribbles, and other metrics that don't directly lead to fantasy points (source: fantasy-optimial-strategy.md).
