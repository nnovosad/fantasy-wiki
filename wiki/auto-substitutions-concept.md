# Automatic Substitutions


**Summary**: The system that replaces non-playing starters with players from the bench at the end of a fantasy round.


**Sources**: [[raw/leagues/england/rules.md]]


**Last updated**: 2026-08-18


---


If a player in the starting XI does not play a single minute in a round, they are automatically replaced by a substitute.


## Rules for Substitutions


- **Priority**: Substitutions are applied based on the order set by the manager (1st, 2nd, and 3rd sub).
- **Goalkeepers**: A non-playing goalkeeper can only be replaced by the backup goalkeeper.
- **Formation Requirements**: Auto-subs must result in a valid formation. The final team must have:
    - 3 to 5 Defenders
    - 2 to 5 Midfielders
    - 1 to 3 Forwards
- **Ineligible Substitutions**: If a substitution would break formation rules (e.g., replacing a defender when only 3 were in the starting XI and the available subs are midfielders/forwards), the points from the substitute will not be counted (source: rules.md).


## Strategic Implication


Because of these rules, the order of the bench is highly important. In OFS, it is recommended to have **15 active players** to ensure that a valid and high-scoring substitute is always available (source: fantasy-optimial-strategy.md).
