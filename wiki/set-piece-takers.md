# Set-Piece Takers


**Summary**: The importance of including players who take penalties, free kicks, and corners in a fantasy squad.


**Sources**: [[fantasy-optimial-strategy.md]]


**Last updated**: 2026-08-18


---


Players who are responsible for set-pieces have a significantly higher probability of scoring goals and providing assists, making them primary targets for any fantasy team (source: fantasy-optimial-strategy.md).


## Penalty Takers


Identifying the designated penalty taker is usually straightforward: the player who has taken the last two penalties is almost certainly the primary choice. Penalty takers are high-value assets because penalties are high-conversion opportunities (source: fantasy-optimial-strategy.md).


## Free Kicks and Corners


While harder to identify from simple statistics, these players provide consistent assist potential and occasional direct goal returns.
