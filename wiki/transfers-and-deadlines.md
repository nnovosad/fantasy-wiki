# Transfers and Deadlines


**Summary**: Rules governing squad changes and the timing of rounds in Sports.ru fantasy football.


**Sources**: [[raw/leagues/england/rules.md]]


**Last updated**: 2026-08-18


---


Managing transfers effectively is a core skill in fantasy football. Decisions must be finalized before the start of each gameweek (source: rules.md).


## Transfer Limits


- **Starting the Season**: Unlimited transfers are allowed until the deadline for the first gameweek.
- **During the Season**: Standard allotment is **3 transfers per gameweek**.
- **Transfer Windows**: The number of transfers may be increased during official transfer windows (e.g., winter break), with announcements typically made via official channels (source: rules.md).
- **Carryover**: Transfers do not carry over; unused transfers are lost (source: fantasy-optimial-strategy.md).


## Deadlines


- **Timing**: The deadline for all changes (transfers, captaincy, bench order) is the **scheduled kickoff time of the first match** of the gameweek.
- **Effect**: Changes made after the deadline only apply to the *following* gameweek (source: rules.md).


## Round Structure


A fantasy gameweek is the period from the start of the first match to the end of the last match. Unlike real-world calendars, **rescheduled matches** are attached to new fantasy rounds to maintain continuous play (source: rules.md).
