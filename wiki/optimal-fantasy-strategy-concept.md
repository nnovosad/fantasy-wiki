# Optimal Fantasy Strategy Concept


**Summary**: The core philosophy of treating fantasy football as a systematic mathematical game, comparable to Game Theory Optimal (GTO) in poker.


**Sources**: [[fantasy-optimial-strategy.md]]


**Last updated**: 2026-08-18


---


The **Optimal Fantasy Strategy (OFS)** is based on the idea that fantasy football is primarily a mathematical game. By mastering key principles and using objective data, a manager can succeed even without watching every match (source: fantasy-optimial-strategy.md).


## Core Principles


- **Systematic Approach**: Moving away from emotional or "fan-based" decisions towards a structured methodology.
- **Mathematical Foundation**: Decisions should be based on probabilities, statistics, and expected value rather than intuition.
- **Universal Applicability**: The principles of OFS are intended to work across various leagues, from the English Premier League to the top league of Gabon (source: fantasy-optimial-strategy.md).


## Comparison to Poker


OFS is explicitly compared to **GTO (Game Theory Optimal)** in poker. Just as poker has extensive didactic material to help players improve, OFS aims to provide a similar educational resource for the fantasy community to help newcomers stay motivated and successful (source: fantasy-optimial-strategy.md).
