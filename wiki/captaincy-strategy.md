# Captaincy Strategy


**Summary**: Criteria for choosing the most effective captain each gameweek to maximize point returns.


**Sources**: [[fantasy-optimial-strategy.md]]


**Last updated**: 2026-08-18


---


The selection of a captain is one of the most important decisions in a fantasy round, as their points are doubled (source: fantasy-optimial-strategy.md).


## Selection Criteria


A captain should generally meet the following four criteria:
1.  **Attacking Player**: In 95% of cases, the captain should be a forward or a midfielder.
2.  **Top Team**: The player should be from one of the top 3 teams favored to score the most goals in the round (based on bookmaker odds).
3.  **Strong Form**: The player should be one of the top performers in their team based on statistics (SpG, KeyP) over the last 3-5 games.
4.  **Reliability**: Avoid players who are likely to be rotated, especially during busy schedules like midweeks (source: fantasy-optimial-strategy.md).


## Differentiating with Captains


If a manager needs to catch up to leaders, they may experiment by captaining a "joker" (a lower-ownership player). However, OFS recommends doing this no more than once every three rounds to maintain stability (source: fantasy-optimial-strategy.md).
