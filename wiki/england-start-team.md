# England Start Team Selection


**Summary**: An optimal starting squad for the English Premier League fantasy season based on OFS principles and pre-season data.


**Sources**: [[england/data_for_pre-season_team_selection.json]], [[england/rules.md]]


**Last updated**: 2026-08-19


---


This squad is designed to maximize point potential while staying within the 100-credit budget and adhering to the 3-player-per-club limit. It follows the **[[optimal-fantasy-strategy-concept]]** by balancing premium "must-haves" with high-value "jokers."


## Squad Composition


### Goalkeepers (10.0 cr)
- **Robin Roefs** (Sunderland) - 5.0 cr
- **Bart Verbruggen** (Brighton) - 5.0 cr
*Strategy: Two mid-priced goalkeepers for rotation and insurance.*


### Defenders (27.5 cr)
- **Nordi Mukiele** (Sunderland) - 5.0 cr
- **Adrien Truffert** (Bournemouth) - 5.5 cr
- **Tyrick Mitchell** (Crystal Palace) - 5.5 cr
- **Marcos Senesi** (Tottenham) - 5.5 cr
- **Jurrien Timber** (Arsenal) - 6.0 cr
*Strategy: Focus on attacking potential and high defensive efficiency from mid-table and top teams.*


### Midfielders (38.0 cr)
- **Bruno Fernandes** (Man Utd) - 11.0 cr (Captain candidate)
- **Elliot Anderson** (Man City) - 7.5 cr
- **James Garner** (Everton) - 6.5 cr
- **Ryan Gravenberch** (Liverpool) - 6.5 cr
- **Granit Xhaka** (Sunderland) - 6.5 cr
*Strategy: Bruno Fernandes provides a premium core, while Anderson and Xhaka offer high-value returns for their prices.*


### Forwards (24.5 cr)
- **Erling Haaland** (Man City) - 11.0 cr (Primary captain candidate)
- **Evanilson** (Bournemouth) - 7.0 cr
- **Danny Welbeck** (Chelsea) - 6.5 cr
*Strategy: Haaland is a "must-have" premium forward. Evanilson and Welbeck provide budget-friendly attacking support.*
